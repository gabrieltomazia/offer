# English: diagnosis questions and scoring rubric

Short English versions of `perguntas-de-diagnostico.md` and `criterios-do-placar.md`.

## Diagnosis questions

Ask one or two at a time. Read the site or profile first and only confirm what you found. Never repeat a question that was already answered.

1. What do you sell, and to whom?
2. One city or region, or fully online?
3. Do you have a website, Instagram, or checkout link I can read?
4. What do you charge today, and per what unit (monthly, per session, one-time)?
5. More than one package? Which sells most?
6. Who is your favorite kind of customer?
7. What does that person want to achieve, in their own words?
8. What proof do you have (testimonials, numbers, years, ratings)? Ask for the concrete figure.
9. Is there a guarantee today? If not, what promise could you keep every single month?
10. What is your real capacity limit (new clients per month, seats, hours)?

Close with a two-line summary and wait for the ok:

> You sell **[what]** to **[whom]** in **[where]**, today at **[price and unit]**. The customer wants **[result]**, and you have **[proof]** to show. Did I get that right?

Never ask for passwords, dashboard access, customers' personal data, or a paid-tool token in the chat.

## Scoring rubric

Score 0 by default. Raise it only with evidence from what is published. When torn between two scores, give the lower one. Use the same ruler for the user and for competitors.

| Key | Question | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `resultado` | Clear result | Only describes the service | Generic benefit | Specific result for a defined audience | Specific, for whom, with a measurable marker |
| `prova` | Proof | None | Loose claim | Checkable proof | Checkable, with numbers, close to the ideal customer |
| `velocidade` | Speed | No timeframe | Vague ("fast") | Defined timeframe | Defined, short for the category, with an early win |
| `facilidade` | Ease | Silent or demanding | Generic support | Removes concrete tasks | Does the hard part for the customer |
| `risco` | Risk removed | Nothing | Only legal rights or a light reducer | Own guarantee with clear terms | Strong, clear guarantee with how to claim |
| `bonus` | Bonuses | None | Generic gift | Tied to the result | Solve specific obstacles, explained |
| `urgencia` | Honest urgency | None, or looks fake | Dated promo, no reason | Limit or date with plausible reason | Real limit, explained, matches capacity |
| `clareza` | Clarity | No name, price, or contents | One of three | Two of three | Specific name, published price, contents list |

Ranking: the current offer and the new offer are each ranked against the competitors. Ties share a position. A gap of one or two points is noise. If the new offer does not reach the top, say so and show what is missing.
